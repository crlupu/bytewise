<p>You can declare <code>T[]</code> and cast an <code>Object[]</code> (with an <button type="button" class="term" aria-expanded="false" data-term="Unchecked warning" data-def="The compiler saying it can&amp;#39;t verify type safety at this point. <p>Arrays are <button type="button" class="term" aria-expanded="false" data-term="Covariant" data-def="If &lt;code&gt;Sub&lt;/code&gt; is a subtype of &lt;code&gt;Super&lt;/code&gt;, then &lt;code&gt;Sub[]&lt;/code&gt; is a subtype of &lt;code&gt;Super[]&lt;/code&gt;.
