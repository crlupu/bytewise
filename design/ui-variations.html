Four ways to build Bytewise

The same three screens, built four ways: different cards, lists, navigation and answer controls. Mix and match: any list can go with any card style.

A

Glow

Dark

Big cards lit from below, horizontally scrolling topic cards, lesson rows with progress rings, and a floating tab bar. Answers are large tiles with A, B, C keys. The most dramatic; closest to Spendvest.

  • Cards: one hero card with a glow; tall topic cards in a carousel
  • Lists: rows with ring, check or lock on the left
  • Lesson: lettered answer tiles, lime Check button
B

Bento

Light

Home is a bento grid of mixed-size tiles: continue, streak, review and accuracy at a glance. The course is a vertical path, and answers sit in a bottom sheet you can reach with one thumb.

  • Cards: bento tiles of different sizes and colours
  • Lists: a connected path of steps; topic rows with thin bars
  • Lesson: question on top, answers in a bottom sheet
C

Editorial

Light

Typography leads. Huge headings, mono labels, numbered lists with hairline dividers, filter chips and segmented progress. Colour appears only for the streak and progress. The calmest and most readable.

  • Cards: almost none; one black streak card
  • Lists: numbered rows, dividers only
  • Lesson: numbered answers, segmented progress bar
D

Glass deck

Dark

A fanned stack of course cards, a "Today's plan" checklist, a big progress ring for each course and lessons as tiles. Answers are pill chips. The most playful and game-like.

  • Cards: a fanned deck; glass tiles in a two-column grid
  • Lists: a checklist with tick boxes
  • Lesson: answer chips, a ring for progress, inline feedback
