Bytewise logo concepts

Six directions in the app's electric blue, each shown as an app icon, at favicon sizes, on black, and next to the wordmark. Recommended: the bitten b.

Recommended

1 · Bitten b

A lowercase b with a bite out of it: bite-sized lessons, and a pun on byte. Simple enough to read at 16 px, and playful without being childish.

Bytewise
Retro

2 · Pixel B

A B drawn as a 5×7 bitmap, like old terminal fonts, with one light-blue pixel as the "spark". Clearly about computers; gets busy below 24 px.

Bytewise
Progress

3 · Steps

Three rising bars and a spark: small steps that add up, like the lessons. Reads as growth; less obviously about code.

Bytewise
Insight

4 · Spark in brackets

Code brackets around a spark: the "aha" moment inside code. Bold and legible at every size, but code-bracket marks are common.

Bytewise
Structure

5 · B-tree

Three linked nodes, each split by a key, echoing the B-tree widget. Hints at structured knowledge; the dividers vanish at 16 px.

Bytewise
Terminal

6 · b_ cursor

A b followed by a blinking cursor, on near-black: "type to begin". Distinctive next to blue icons; the cursor could animate in the app.

Today

Current · Byte of tiles

The icon in the app now: eight tiles for eight bits, two dimmed. Fine as a placeholder, but abstract — it doesn't say "learning".
