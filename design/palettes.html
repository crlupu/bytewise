Bytewise colour palettes

Four directions built from navy, green and amber. Each is shown on a lesson screen in light and dark mode, with the b-and-spark logo recoloured. Contrast is checked against WCAG AA: 4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for controls.

Navy

Midnight

Navy does everything: buttons, selection, progress and the tick on a right answer. Amber only lights the spark and the streak. Calm and serious, like a good textbook.
